### 🧭 About Me

I'm exploring the practical side of **Generative AI** — building agents, retrieval pipelines, and applied ML systems rather than just experimenting with prompts. My projects lean toward things that actually *do* something: answer questions over documents, query databases in natural language, or recommend a movie you'll actually like.

- 🔭 Currently building agentic and RAG-based applications
- 🌱 Learning: advanced RAG architectures, multi-agent orchestration, and LLM tooling
- 💬 Ask me about: LangChain, RAG pipelines, OCR-based document search, recommender systems
